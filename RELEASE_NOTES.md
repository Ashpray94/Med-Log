MedLog VERSION makes doctor summaries easier to read and adds medicine and feed editing. Install over the existing app to preserve records.

## What's new
- **Simple symptom totals.** Times noted means the number of symptom notes in the selected period. Daily graphs and last-noted times match the dated entries. Improvement updates are separate.
- **Detailed PDF.** Each symptom includes a daily graph, dated entries, remarks and individual body maps. Recorded depth appears in entry details when supplied.
- **Clear body locations.** Numbered labels connect to their recorded positions; individual entry maps remain available by opening a symptom.
- **Edit medicines and feeds.** Change names, doses or amounts, times, days, dates and feed ingredients; pause or remove entries while keeping past dose history.
- **Preserved feed history.** Dose records retain feed details so later recipe edits do not recalculate past nutrition using the new recipe.
- **Safe database upgrade.** Existing records migrate to the new history format.

Counts describe recorded notes, not independently confirmed episodes. This update does not invent missing medication confirmations.

Install MedLog-VERSION-arm64.apk on most phones; use the armv7 APK for older phones.
