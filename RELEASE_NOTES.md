Updates straight over 2.10 and keeps your data.

## What's new
- **Messages on the helper's page:** a tinted strip says who and when, the message in large words, then the answers. Once answered, a thin banner at the bottom says what you said.
- **Medicines and feeds apart** on the helper's page, each with its own "See all".
- **Feed history on the Feeds page:** each day once, each feed named once, with its times: given, food instead, not given, missed.
- **Missed feeds** on Nutrition: each feed named once with its missed times by day.
- **Details on the left** of every medicine card; the answer on the right.
- Cards have a faint edge as well as the soft shadow, so they stand apart from the page.

Install **MedLog-VERSION-arm64.apk** on most phones.
