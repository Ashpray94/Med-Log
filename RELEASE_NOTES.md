Updates straight over 2.9 and keeps your data.

## What's new
- **Helpers can do everything for the person.** Note how they feel, their food, toilet and medicines; see the same history and doctor page. Their latest message is the first thing on the helper's page.
- **Alarms stop when you swipe them.** A swiped or answered reminder goes quiet at once, and a quiet reminder stays until it's answered. When one helper answers, the other phones go quiet too.
- **Loud or quiet reminders.** Medicines and messages from the person ring; food, feeds and water are quiet notifications.
- **Note things later.** "When did you take it?" for a late dose, change the time of any dose, and fix any day's medicines from History.
- **One card per medicine.** Its times sit on one line ("12 PM · 6 PM · +2 more"); tap for every dose.
- **Records reach helpers by Bluetooth** when the internet can't.
- **SOS: say what's happening.** Pictures under Call 108 tell your family what kind of emergency it is.
- **Two new widgets:** How I feel, and Toilet & vomit.
- **Easier to read.** No word is ever cut off or split across lines, in any language or word size. Stronger outlines on everything you can tap. Plain words: Done, Remove, Note.
- **Hindi and Tamil everywhere,** with dates in your language.
- Nothing is saved to your phone's files except the doctor page when you share or print it.

Install **MedLog-VERSION-arm64.apk** on most phones.
